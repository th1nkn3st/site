# Crownlines

<p class="cl-tagline">Outthink. Capture. Reign.</p>

Crownlines is a two-player strategy board game based on Kharbaga, a traditional
capture-and-crown game. Captures are compulsory and can chain, and pieces that
reach the far side are crowned as Mullas.

## What's in the game

- **Two rulesets.** Play on the fast 5×5 board, or on the traditional board with 20 pieces per side.
- **Five AI levels.** They run from Novice to Master.
- **Pass & Play.** Play a friend on one device, with an optional board flip.
- **The Sunken Crown campaign.** 12 stages across three chapters, each with its own twist.
- **Puzzles.** 16 hand-made tactical puzzles.
- **An interactive tutorial.** It teaches moving, capturing, chaining, and crowning on a live board.
- **Cosmetic piece sets and board themes.** You buy them with coins earned in play. There are no ads and no real-money purchases.
