# Privacy Policy

**App:** Crownlines (Android package `com.th1nkn3st.crownlines`)<br>
**Developer:** {{ site.title }}<br>
**Effective date:** October 6, 2026

<div class="cl-note">
<strong>In short:</strong> Crownlines does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any
personal data. It has no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking, and it does not use
the internet.
</div>

## Information we collect

None. Crownlines does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos, or any
other personal information. It contains no advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting
software, and it does not request internet access.

## Information stored on your device

To work properly, Crownlines saves a small amount of game data in the app's private
storage on your device:

| Data | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Settings, such as sound, music, haptics, theme, and text size | Remembers your preferences |
| Progress: coins, campaign and puzzle stars, local ratings, badges, and owned cosmetics | Keeps your progress between sessions |
| The current match against the AI or in Pass & Play | Lets you continue where you left off |

This data never leaves your device. We cannot see it or access it. You can delete
it at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling
the app.

## Permissions

Crownlines requests one Android permission:

- **Vibrate.** It is used for short haptic feedback during play. You can turn haptics
  off in the game's Settings.

## Google Play

Crownlines is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information
when you download or use apps, under the
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). As the developer, we
may see aggregated, anonymous statistics that Google provides through the Play
Console, such as install counts and crash rates. These statistics do not identify you.

## Children

Crownlines is not directed at children under 13. Because the app collects no personal
data from anyone, it collects none from children.

## This website

addresses, for security and operations, under the
[GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
The Crownlines pages on this site use no cookies and no analytics.
