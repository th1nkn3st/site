One board. A different story every match.

Inspired by Kharbaga, the classic capture-and-crown duel. Learn the essentials with guided practice, then find your own way across the board.

  1. 01

    Find your move.

    Move forward to build your position. When a capture is available, you must take it.

  2. 02

    Follow the chain.

    Jump your opponent's pieces. Keep capturing when another jump opens up.

  3. 03

    Claim the crown.

    Reach the far side to become a Mulla, with movement and captures in any direction.
