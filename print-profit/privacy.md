# Print Profit privacy policy

Updated October 20, 2026. Print Profit is a 3D-print pricing calculator published by th1nkn3st. Contact: [hello@th1nkn3st.com](mailto:hello@th1nkn3st.com).

## Your models, images and prices
Models, notes, prices, printer settings and preferences are stored locally on your device. th1nkn3st does not operate a server collecting those records. There is no app account or advertising SDK. Android cloud backup is disabled in the launch build.

Imported images and recognized text are processed on-device by Google ML Kit. They are not sent to Google servers for recognition. The app holds the selected image and scan text in memory during the current app session; it does not intentionally save an image library. Images you selected or captured may also remain in their original photo or file location, controlled by Android or your chosen app.

## SDK diagnostics
Google's ML Kit SDK collects device and application information, per-installation identifiers, performance metrics, API configuration, input/output sizes, SDK feature versions, event types and error codes for diagnostics and usage analytics. These are SDK metadata, not the image contents or recognized text. Google states this data is encrypted in transit and is not transferred by ML Kit to third parties. See [ML Kit's Android data disclosure](https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure) and [ML Kit terms and privacy](https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms).

Print Profit does not include separate studio analytics or an active market-data sharing program.

## Purchases
Google Play processes the optional one-time Pro purchase and manages purchase records and account information under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Print Profit checks the local Play purchase entitlement to unlock features. th1nkn3st does not receive your card details. Purchases and restoration require a connection to Google Play.

## Exports and support
CSV exports are created locally. On Android you choose the destination in the share sheet; that destination's policies then apply. When you email support, we receive the email address, message and attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond and troubleshoot, and keep correspondence only as needed for those purposes and applicable obligations. Request deletion through the same support address.

## Deletion
Clearing app storage or uninstalling deletes local app data. Exports and original images elsewhere must be deleted separately. Contact Google about Play purchase records and SDK data under its policies; clearing this app's data does not erase Google's records.

## Children and changes
This is a business productivity tool, not designed or marketed for children under 13. Material changes will be reflected here before a changed data practice launches. No optional future data collection is enabled by the current release.
