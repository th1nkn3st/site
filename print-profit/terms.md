## Free and Pro
The free version includes one custom printer profile, ten saved models and attributed exports. Pro unlocks unlimited models and custom profiles and removes export attribution through a one-time Google Play purchase. There is no recurring subscription. Final price and applicable taxes appear in Google's purchase sheet. Purchases, refunds and restoration are subject to applicable Google Play terms and your statutory rights. Restore with the original Google Play account. Buying Pro does not include cloud storage, synchronization, a market-data service or a promise of future features.
