# Print Profit support

Contact [hello@th1nkn3st.com](mailto:hello@th1nkn3st.com?subject=Print%20Profit%20support) with your app version, Android version, device and steps to reproduce a problem. Do not send payment details or confidential customer data.

## Where are my models stored?
On this device. Export a CSV before uninstalling or clearing storage. CSV is a spreadsheet export, not an app backup or restore format. Android cloud backup is disabled in the launch build.

## Why does a screenshot need review?
OCR can confuse numbers or read totals from multiple plates. Check grams, duration and plate count against the slicer. Use manual entry if recognition is uncertain.

## Does changing currency convert my costs?
No. It changes the currency used to display amounts. Enter every price and cost in that currency. Electricity presets are starter estimates originally in USD/kWh; use the actual rate on your bill.

## How does Pro work?
Pro is a one-time purchase through Google Play, with no subscription. It unlocks unlimited saved models and custom printers, and removes export attribution. Use **Restore purchases** in Settings with the same Google Play account after reinstalling. Purchases require an internet connection. If pricing has not loaded, try again with Play Store connectivity.

## Why are estimates different from actual profit?
The calculator uses entered costs, fees and operating assumptions. It does not predict demand or account for taxes, shipping or other costs you have not entered. Add hands-on work minutes per model and an hourly labor rate in Settings. Capacity assumes the printer runs the configured hours and every produced unit sells.
